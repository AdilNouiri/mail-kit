## D2 — Size tiers (completes F4.7)

Tiers by displayed length n: n ≤ 12 is 30/36 px with 8 px spacing (unchanged); 13 to 16 is
22/28 px with 4 px; 17 to 23 is 16/22 px with 2 px. Tiers above 12 add `white-space:nowrap`.
Line heights (28 and 22) are the spec's "scaled to match".
